### 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Product & Data Analyst** who lives at the intersection of user behavior, business strategy, and data pipelines. I turn messy data, user signals, and business questions into product decisions — whether that means building a metrics dashboard, running an A/B test, or shipping a RAG-powered chatbot end to end.

- 🔍 **Product side:** funnel analysis, A/B testing, roadmap prioritization, user research, PRDs, metrics frameworks (AARRR, HEART)
- 📊 **Data side:** SQL, Python, statistical analysis, dashboarding, ML-lite (embeddings, retrieval, LLM pipelines)
- 🤖 **Currently building:** RAG systems using LangChain, ChromaDB & LLMs — bridging "what happened" with "what should we do about it"
- 🌱 **Currently exploring:** LLM evaluation frameworks & agentic workflows for analytics
- 💬 **Ask me about:** product metrics, SQL, dashboards, chatbot/RAG architecture, A/B testing
- 📍 Based in **Bangalore, India**
